### 👋 About me

I'm a music technology researcher working at the intersection of **music information retrieval, recommendation, and human–AI interaction**. I build datasets and models for music that current systems tend to overlook, such as *pansori*, a Korean vocal tradition. I also study how people describe, perceive, and create music alongside AI.

- 🎓 M.S. in Music Technology, **Georgia Tech** (2026) · B.S. in Music Industry, minor in CS, **Drexel** (2024)
- 🔬 Currently researching with the MALer Lab (Sogang), Prof. Hao-Wen Dong (UMich), and the MAC Lab (KAIST)
- 🎯 **Applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027**
- 📝 Reviewer for ISMIR 2026 and ICASSP 2027
